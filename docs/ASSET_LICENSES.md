# Asset Provenance & License Ledger

**Every non-code asset in this repo must have a row here.** This is our proof of ownership for Steam/Epic (both require you to warrant you hold all rights) and our defense against any future claim. A PR adding art/audio without a ledger row should not be merged.

## Rules (read before adding any asset)

1. **Commissioned work** (artists, musicians): a **written IP-assignment agreement must be signed before work begins.** By default the *creator* owns the copyright — not whoever paid. No contract, no merge. Store signed contracts outside the repo (they contain personal data); link the reference here.
2. **Music has two copyrights** — the composition *and* the recording. A public-domain folk rhythm does not make someone's recording of it free. Only use: our own recordings, commissioned recordings (with contract), or marketplace licenses (keep the receipt + license text).
3. **Marketplace/stock assets**: license must explicitly allow commercial video-game use and redistribution inside a game binary. Save the license PDF/receipt. Avoid CC-BY-SA (copyleft) and "personal use" tiers. CC0 is ideal; CC-BY needs the attribution kept in `CREDITS`.
4. **AI-generated/assisted assets are allowed but must be marked** in the ledger — Steam requires AI disclosure on the store page, and we must be able to fill that questionnaire accurately.
5. **Fonts** need licenses too — OFL (SIL Open Font License) or equivalent only.
6. **Never**: YouTube/game rips, "found on the internet," fan art of existing IP, uncredited pack shares from forums/Discord.

## When real assets replace placeholders

Add a row per batch with: who made it, under what agreement (link the contract reference, e.g. "Agreement #003, signed 2026-08-01"), and the license granted. For percussion recordings, note the performer, instrument, and that both composition (traditional/original) and recording rights are assigned.
